Extended CryEngine with custom lighting, shadowing, post-processing, and surface-detail technology built for a dark post-apocalyptic world.

I developed advanced rendering systems for Russia 2028, including Parallax Decals, Normal Ambient Light, improved shadow cascades, point-light shadows, particle effects, and post-processing. The work went beyond visual tuning-I modified core rendering behavior to give the project a distinct atmosphere and stronger environmental depth.

Working closely with the art director, I designed and implemented Normal Ambient Light, a custom shading technique created to preserve form and detail in large outdoor environments without breaking the game's heavily desaturated visual style.

I also reworked shadow rendering and optimized the screen-space ambient occlusion pipeline, improving contrast, depth, and readability across dark scenes while maintaining real-time performance.

One of the project's defining systems was Parallax Decals, which added physical depth to damaged walls, crumbling plaster, rubble, dust-covered surfaces, and dirty windows without relying on expensive geometry.

The result was a major expansion of CryEngine's rendering capabilities, shaped specifically around the visual identity of the game rather than limited by the engine's default pipeline.
